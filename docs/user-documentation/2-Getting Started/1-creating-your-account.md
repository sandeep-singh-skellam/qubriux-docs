# Creating Your Account

Welcome to Qubriux! We're excited to help you grow your business. The first step is to create your account. This guide will walk you through the simple sign-up process.

### Step 1: Visit the Sign-Up Page

Navigate to the [Qubriux Sign-Up Page](https://app.qubriux.com/auth/signup). You'll see a form asking for your basic information.

### Step 2: Fill in Your Details

1.  **Full Name:** Enter your first and last name.
2.  **Email Address:** Use your primary business email. This will be your username.
3.  **Password:** Choose a strong, secure password.


### Step 3: Verify Your Email

After submitting the form, we will send a verification link to the email address you provided. Click this link to activate your account.

That's it! Your account is now active, and you can log in to the Qubriux dashboard.
