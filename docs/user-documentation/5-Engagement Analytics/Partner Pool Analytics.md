# Partner Pool Analytics

Partner Pool Analytics tracks the performance of your [coupon pools](../3-Core%20Concepts/Partner%20Pools/1-partner-pools.md) - showing how many coupons have been issued, redeemed, and how quickly customers are redeeming them after receipt.

## Overall Pool Performance

The **Overall** view shows aggregate metrics across all partner pools as of a selected date.

1. Navigate to **Engagement Analytics > Partner Pool Analytics**.
2. Select an **As Of Date** to view a point-in-time snapshot.
3. Optionally filter to a specific **Coupon Pool** to scope the data.

Metrics shown include:
- Total coupons in the pool
- Coupons issued to customers
- Coupons redeemed
- Redemption rate
- Remaining (unredeemed) coupons

## Redemption Curve

The **Redemption Curve** shows how quickly customers redeem coupons after they are issued - helping you understand the urgency and effectiveness of your coupon campaigns.

Metrics include:

- **Average Time to Redemption** - the average number of seconds between a coupon being issued and it being redeemed. A lower value indicates customers are acting quickly on the offer.
- **Redemption Curve Data** - a time-series breakdown showing the cumulative redemption rate at different intervals after issuance (e.g. within 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days).

:::tip
If your redemption curve shows most redemptions happening within the first few hours, your coupons have strong urgency. If the curve is flat, consider adding expiry dates or push notification reminders to drive faster redemption.
:::
