# Badge Analytics

Badge Analytics shows you how customers are earning and accumulating badges across your gamification programme - both at the overall portfolio level and for each individual badge.

## Overall Badge Performance

The **Overall** view shows aggregate badge metrics across all badges for a selected date range.

1. Navigate to **Engagement Analytics > Badge Analytics**.
2. Set a **From Date** and **To Date**.
3. View total badges awarded, unique customers who earned at least one badge, and award trends over the period.

## Badge-Level Analytics

Drill into any specific badge to see its individual performance:

1. Navigate to **Engagement Analytics > Badge Analytics**.
2. Select a badge from the list.
3. Set a date range using **From Date** and **To Date**.

Badge-level analytics show:
- Total awards for that badge in the period
- Unique customers who received it
- Award trend over time

:::tip
Use badge-level analytics to identify which badges are rarely earned - they may indicate goals that are set too high - and which are awarded too frequently, which can dilute their perceived value.
:::
