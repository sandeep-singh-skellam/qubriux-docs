# Challenge Analytics

Challenge Analytics gives you visibility into how your gamification challenges are performing - how many customers are participating, how they are progressing through challenge milestones, and which KPIs are being met.

## Overall Challenge Performance

The **Overall** view shows aggregate challenge metrics across all challenges for a selected date range.

1. Navigate to **Engagement Analytics > Challenge Analytics**.
2. Set a **Start Date** and **End Date**.
3. View merchant-level metrics covering all active and completed challenges.

## Analytics Configuration

The **Analytics Config** view shows the KPI definitions and measurement settings configured for your challenges - for example, which events count as completions, what thresholds trigger a reward, and how progress is calculated. This helps you verify that your challenge logic is set up correctly before drawing conclusions from the data.

## Exporting Customers by KPI

You can export a list of customers filtered by a specific challenge KPI - for example, all customers who completed a challenge, or all customers who reached a specific milestone but did not complete.

1. Open the Challenge Analytics view.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Select the KPI type to filter by.
4. Confirm - the customer list is generated and made available for download.

This export is useful for building follow-up segments, recognising completers with a reward, or re-engaging customers who dropped off mid-challenge.
