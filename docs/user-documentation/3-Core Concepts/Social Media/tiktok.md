# TikTok

Qubriux integrates with TikTok for Business to let you export customer segments as Custom Audiences and target them with ads on TikTok. Once your TikTok account is connected and an advertiser account is selected, you can push any Qubriux segment directly to TikTok Ads Manager.

## Connecting Your TikTok Account

1. Navigate to **Social Media > TikTok**.
2. Click **Connect TikTok Account**.
3. You will be redirected to TikTok's OAuth login page. Log in with the TikTok account linked to your TikTok for Business / Ads Manager account.
4. Approve the requested permissions and return to Qubriux.
5. Qubriux exchanges the authorisation code for an access token and saves it securely.

:::note
You need a TikTok for Business account with Ads Manager access to connect. A standard TikTok creator account cannot be used.
:::

## Setting Up Your Advertiser Account

After connecting your TikTok account, you need to select which TikTok Advertiser account Qubriux should use for audience exports.

1. Once connected, Qubriux fetches the list of Advertiser accounts associated with your TikTok login.
2. Select the **Advertiser Account** you want to link.
3. Click **Save**.

:::tip
If you manage multiple brands, each can be linked to a separate TikTok Advertiser account by setting up each Qubriux merchant account independently.
:::

## Exporting a Segment to TikTok

Once your account is connected and your advertiser is configured, you can export any customer segment to TikTok as a Custom Audience.

1. Navigate to **Segments** and open the segment you want to export.
2. Click **Export to Social Media** and select **TikTok**.
3. Choose **Create New Audience** to push the segment as a new Custom Audience in TikTok Ads Manager.
4. Click **Export**.

Qubriux sends the customer list to TikTok. Once processed, the audience becomes available in TikTok Ads Manager for use in campaigns.

:::note
TikTok requires a minimum audience size before a Custom Audience can be used in ad targeting. If your segment is too small, the audience will be created but may not be immediately targetable.
:::

## Managing Custom Audiences

You can view all TikTok Custom Audiences linked to your merchant account:

1. Navigate to **Social Media > TikTok > Audiences**.
2. The list shows all audiences previously exported from Qubriux to TikTok.

To view audiences linked to a specific segment:

1. Open the segment from the **Segments** page.
2. Click **View Audiences** to see TikTok audiences associated with that segment.

## Disconnecting TikTok

To remove your TikTok connection, contact your Qubriux administrator. Removing the connection will stop future audience exports but will not affect existing audiences or active campaigns in TikTok Ads Manager.
