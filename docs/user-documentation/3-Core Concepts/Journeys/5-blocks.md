# Adding Blocks
To add more elements to your strategy, click on the plus icon. Once clicked, the right pane opens up with a list of options:
* Add If/Else Branch
* Audience Split
* Add Time Delay
* Send Email
* Send WhatsApp
* Send SMS
* Send Offer
* A/B Testing
* Send Survey
* Social Media Ads
* Loyalty Node
* Award Loyalty Points
* Send Push Notification
* Add Game

You can add multiple blocks one after another or insert them at any point in the journey by clicking the plus icon.


![image info](/img/journeys/5.1-journeys.png)


## Adding an if/else branch

Adding a branch creates two distinct customizable parallel paths in your strategy. You can use branching to reach out to your customers in different ways. If the branching condition holds true, eligible customers follow the Yes path, and the rest of the customers follow the No path. Do mention the time within which you wish the event should happen. 

![image info](/img/journeys/5.2-journeys.png)

You can add blocks on the Yes and No path as per your flow. 

![image info](/img/journeys/5.3-journeys.png)

## Adding Audience Split
Qubriux Journey now allow you to split the total target audience of a journey into separate paths based on segment or any other custom filter you may need. Each audience split can have independent journey paths created.

* While adding a node to Journey, select Audience Split Node

![image info](/img/journeys/5.4-journeys.png)

* Give a group name and select the segments to target in this split. 

![image info](/img/journeys/5.5-journeys.png)

* Audience Split is now ready. All customers who qualify your filters will follow the “Bronze Tier” path. All remaining customers       
       will follow the “Everyone Else” path

![image info](/img/journeys/5.6-journeys.png)


## Adding Time Delay 
Adding a delay creates a waiting period between the previous block and the next block. You can set a delay of days, hours, minutes, or any combination of these. If blocks are back-to-back without delay, then they occur sequentially without any wait if they qualify. 

![image info](/img/journeys/5.7-journeys.png)

## Adding A/B Testing 

A/B testing, also known as split testing, is a method used in journey builder to compare two different variations of a campaign element and determine which one performs better. The goal of A/B testing is to optimize Journey effectiveness by making data-driven decisions.

Here's how A/B testing works in a campaign:

1. **Define your objective**: Clearly identify the goal you want to achieve through your journey. It could be increasing click-through rates, improving conversion rates, or maximising sales, among others.
2. **Identify the element to test**: Choose a specific element of your journey  that you want to test. 
3. **Create two versions**: Develop two distinct variations of the chosen element. These versions, often referred to as A and B, should differ in only one aspect. 
4. **Split your audience**: Randomly divide your target audience into two or more than two groups. Group A will be exposed to version A, while group B will be exposed to version B and so on. It is essential to ensure that these groups are representative of your overall target audience.
5. **Run the test**: Launch your journey and measure the performance of each version. Track relevant metrics based on your objective.
6. **Analyse the results**: Compare the performance of version A and version B using the collected data. Determine which version outperformed the other based on the established metrics. Statistical analysis can help validate the significance of the differences observed.
7. **Draw conclusions**: Based on the results, draw conclusions about the effectiveness of the tested element. Decide whether one version significantly outperformed the other or if the results are inconclusive.
8. **Implement the winning version**: If a significant difference is observed, implement the winning version as the default choice in your journey. This ensures that you are continuously improving your marketing efforts based on data-driven insights.
9. **Iterative testing**: A/B testing is an iterative process. Once you have implemented the winning version, you can continue testing other elements or further refine the chosen element to optimize your campaign even more.

Remember, A/B testing should be conducted in a controlled manner, ensuring that only one variable is changed between the versions. This allows you to isolate the impact of that specific element on the journey's performance. Additionally, it's important to test a sufficiently large sample size to obtain reliable results.

By employing A/B testing in your campaigns, you can make informed decisions and continually improve your marketing strategies based on real data rather than relying solely on assumptions or intuition.
