# Creating a Badge

This guide walks you through setting up a badge in Qubriux - from uploading visuals to configuring rewards and assignment limits.

Badges are created once and then used across your [challenges](../Challenges/1-challenges.md), [journeys](../Journeys/1-Journeys.md), and loyalty rules. A well-configured badge in your library can be added to as many programmes as you like without needing to be recreated.

---

## Step 1 - Open the Badges Section

Navigate to **Badges** in the main menu. Your badge library shows all existing badges and how many customers have earned each one. Click **Create Badge** to begin.

---

## Step 2 - Upload Badge Visuals

A badge's visual identity is what makes it feel worth earning. Upload the assets that will represent this badge to customers:

**Badge image**
The icon displayed on a customer's profile when they've earned the badge. This is the primary visual - make it distinctive. Customers will see this alongside all their other badges, so it should stand out and be immediately recognisable.

**Banner image**
The larger image shown when a customer earns the badge or taps to view it in the app. This is the "moment of achievement" visual - it should feel rewarding and celebrate the milestone.

Images are uploaded directly through the badge creation form. Supported format: PNG.

---

## Step 3 - Name and Describe the Badge

**Badge name**
The name customers see. Keep it short and evocative - "Gold Member," "Coffee Connoisseur," "Big Spender," "Founding Member." The name should communicate instantly what the badge represents.

**Description**
A short explanation of what the badge means and how it was earned. For example: "Awarded to customers who have spent over $1,000 in a single transaction." Customers see this when they view the badge on their profile.

---

## Step 4 - Choose the Badge Type

Select the type that fits how this badge should behave:

**One-Time**
A recognition badge. It is awarded once (or up to your per-customer limit) and stays on the customer's profile permanently as a mark of achievement. Use this for milestones, achievements, and status markers.

**Boost Effect**
An active badge that applies a multiplier or benefit while the customer holds it. Use this for badges that come with a tangible advantage - a points multiplier, an enhanced earn rate, or any boost that is active for as long as the badge is held.

---

## Step 5 - Add Rewards (Optional)

Configure what the customer automatically receives the moment this badge is awarded. You can attach one or more rewards:

**Loyalty Points**
Enter the number of points to credit to the customer's wallet when the badge is awarded. Points are delivered instantly.

**Offer**
Select an [offer](../Offers/1-offers.md) from your Offers library. The offer is sent to the customer automatically at the moment the badge is awarded.

**Cashback**
Enter the cashback amount to credit to the customer's wallet when the badge is awarded.

You can combine all three - a badge can deliver points, an offer, and cashback simultaneously.

If you don't want the badge to carry a reward - for example, if it's a pure recognition badge - simply leave this section blank.

---

## Step 6 - Set the Per-Customer Assignment Limit

Control how many times a single customer can receive this badge:

Leave this blank if there is no limit - customers can earn the badge every time it is triggered (for example, every time they complete a repeating streak challenge).

Set a number if you want to cap it. For example:
- **1** - the customer can only ever earn this badge once, no matter how many programmes reference it
- **3** - the customer can earn it up to three times, receiving the attached reward each time

---

## Step 7 - Set a Global Assignment Limit / Budget (Optional)

Set a total cap on how many times this badge can be awarded across all customers combined. Once this limit is reached, no further customers can earn the badge.

Use this when scarcity is part of the badge's appeal ("only the first 500 customers"), or to cap the total cost of the attached rewards.

Enter the total number of badges that can be issued in the **Budget** section.

---

## Step 8 - Set a Segment (Optional)

Restrict which customers are eligible to receive this badge by selecting a [segment](../Segments/Segments.md). Even if the badge is referenced inside a challenge or journey that reaches a broader audience, only customers who fall within the segment can be awarded it.

Leave this blank to make the badge available to all customers.

---

## Step 9 - Add Alias Details (Optional)

If you operate in multiple markets or languages, configure alternate versions of the badge for different regions:

**Alias name**
An alternate badge name displayed to customers in the relevant market.

**Alias description**
An alternate description for the same badge.

**Alias banner image**
An alternate banner image - useful if different markets require different visual assets or language on the banner.

The correct version is shown automatically based on the customer's context.

---

## Step 10 - Add a Deep Link URL (Optional)

Set a destination URL that opens when a customer taps the badge in the app. This could link to:
- The challenge or journey the badge was earned from
- A relevant product or category page
- A special landing page for badge earners

Leave blank if you don't need the badge to link anywhere specific.

---

## Step 11 - Save the Badge

Click **Save** to add the badge to your library. The badge is now active and ready to be used in challenges, journeys, and loyalty rules.

There is no separate "activate" step - a saved badge is immediately available to reference in any programme.

---

## Editing a Badge

You can update a badge's name, description, images, rewards, limits, and segment at any time from the badge detail page. Changes apply to future awards - customers who have already earned the badge are not affected.

---

## Deleting a Badge

A badge cannot be deleted if it is currently referenced in an active Journey or Challenge. You will be shown which active programmes are using it. Remove the badge from those programmes first, then delete it.

---

## Awarding a Badge Manually

To award a badge directly to a specific customer without going through a challenge or journey, use the **Award Badge** option from the badge detail page. Enter the customer's ID, email address, or mobile number, and the badge will be awarded immediately.

This is useful for:
- Recognising a customer for something that happened outside your standard programmes
- Correcting a missed award
- One-off VIP gestures

---

## Viewing Customer Badges

From any customer's profile, you can view all the badges they have earned - including which badge it is, when it was awarded, and which programme or team member awarded it.

---

## Monitoring Badge Performance

From the badge library, each badge shows how many unique customers have earned it. Click into an individual badge for deeper analytics:

- Total number of times the badge has been awarded
- Unique customers who hold it
- The first 10 customers to earn it
- Average time from programme start to badge earned
- Earning trends over time
